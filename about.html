About us

Our story begins on November 17, 2026

The South Jersey Filipino-American Chess Club was founded to give our community a place to play, learn and grow — together, over the board.

Who we are

A home for chess in South Jersey

SJFACC is a community chess club rooted in the Filipino-American tradition of family, hospitality and friendly competition. We welcome Filipino-American players of every age and strength — from those learning how the pieces move to experienced tournament players.

The club began as the idea of Andrew Suganob, a lifelong chess player. With the support of the Filipino American Society of South Jersey (FASSJ), through Heidi Italia, he was introduced to fellow player Alex Madriaga, and together they held the club’s first meeting on November 17, 2026.

Our goal is to bring the community together over the board, grow toward tournaments, and discover the next generation of talent.

Our mission

To grow a welcoming, well-run chess community that develops players, celebrates Filipino-American heritage and brings families together.
