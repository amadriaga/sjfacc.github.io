About us

Our story begins on November 17, 2026

The South Jersey Filipino-American Chess Club was founded to give our community a place to play, learn and grow — together, over the board.

Who we are

A home for chess in South Jersey

SJFACC is a community chess club rooted in the Filipino-American tradition of family, hospitality and friendly competition. We welcome players of every background, age and strength — from those learning how the pieces move to experienced tournament players.

We were founded on November 17, 2026, and we are building the club with our first members. The programs, events and culture of SJFACC will be shaped by the people who join now.

Our mission

To grow a welcoming, well-run chess community that develops players, celebrates Filipino-American heritage and brings families together.

What we value

The principles we play by

  • 01

    Respect

    Good sportsmanship at every board — a handshake before and after every game.

  • 02

    Growth

    Every member improves — through practice, study, coaching and honest analysis.

  • 03

    Community

    Chess as a meeting place for families, friends and generations.

  • 04

    Heritage

    Proud of our Filipino-American roots, and open to everyone who loves the game.
