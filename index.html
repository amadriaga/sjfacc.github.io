What we do

A club built around the board

Three commitments shape everything we organise — from casual club nights to rated competition.

♞

Play

Regular club nights with casual, rapid and blitz games — and, as the club grows, rated play for members who want to compete.

♝

Learn

Lessons, game analysis and a junior program — structured improvement for newcomers and experienced players alike.

♚

Community

A welcoming home for Filipino-American families and every chess lover in South Jersey — friendships made across the board.
