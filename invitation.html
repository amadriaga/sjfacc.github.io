The evening

Something for every player

Whether you’re a tournament regular or learning how the pieces move, there’s a place for you at the board.

♛

The Founders’ Cup

A friendly three-round tournament — everyone plays every round. A trophy for the club’s first champion, and medals.

♟

Beginners’ & Juniors’ Corner

New to chess? A lesson on the basics and friendly games, with a coach close at hand.

★

Merienda

Filipino snacks, good company and conversation between rounds.
