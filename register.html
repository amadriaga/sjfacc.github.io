Become a founding member

Online registration opens Monday, November 23, 2026, here and on our Facebook page. Complete the membership form and waiver below, the same one we use on paper, and we'll be in touch with membership details.

This is the same form as our printable membership form and waiver (PDF). Fields marked * are required.

Member Information

Chess experience *

1Participation Agreement

I voluntarily join the South Jersey Filipino-American Chess Club (SJFACC) and agree to take part in its activities, including club nights, meetings, lessons, tournaments and other club events. I understand that taking part means following the club’s rules, respecting other members and showing good sportsmanship.

2Assumption of Risk

Chess is a low-risk activity, but I understand that club activities may still involve some risks, such as slips or falls at the venue, accidents while traveling to and from events, and loss of or damage to personal belongings. I voluntarily accept these risks.

3Release of Liability

To the fullest extent allowed by New Jersey law, I release and hold harmless the South Jersey Filipino-American Chess Club, its founders, officers and volunteers, the Filipino American Society of South Jersey (FASSJ), The Church of the Good Shepherd Cherry Hill, and the club’s partner organizations from any claims for injury, loss or damage arising from my participation in club activities, including claims of ordinary negligence. This release does not cover gross negligence or intentional misconduct.

4Code of Conduct

I agree to:

  • Treat all members, instructors, organizers and guests with respect.
  • Follow the club’s rules and the instructions of club officers and volunteers.
  • Play fairly and honestly: no phones, computers or outside help during games.
  • Show good sportsmanship, win or lose.
  • Take care of chess equipment, club property and the church premises, and follow the venue’s rules.
  • Avoid disruptive, unsafe or inappropriate behavior, including harassment or bullying.

I understand that the club may suspend or end the membership of anyone who seriously or repeatedly breaks this code.

5Emergency Medical Consent

If the member needs urgent medical care and the emergency contact cannot be reached, I authorize the club’s officers or volunteers to call 911 and arrange emergency treatment. I am responsible for any resulting medical costs.

6Photos and Videos

The club may take photos and videos at club activities and use them on its website, Facebook page and printed materials.

The information on this form is used only for club purposes: membership records, contacting members and emergencies. 9Acknowledgment and Consent

I have read and understand this form. I agree to its terms and sign it freely.

Parent / Legal Guardian Consent (members under 18)

I am the parent or legal guardian of the member named above. I give permission for my child to take part in club activities. I have read and understand this form and agree to its terms on my child’s behalf, to the extent allowed by law. I am responsible for supervising my child before, during and after club activities.
